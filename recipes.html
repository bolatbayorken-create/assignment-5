Breakfast

Fluffy Pancakes

Soft golden pancakes for a cozy morning.

20 min Easy 2 servings
View full recipe

Ingredients

  • 125 g all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 180 ml milk
  • 1 tbsp melted butter, plus extra for the pan
  • Berries or honey to serve

Instructions

  1. Combine flour, sugar and baking powder in a bowl.
  2. Whisk the egg, milk and melted butter in another bowl.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and mix gently.
  4. Heat a lightly buttered pan over medium heat and add small portions of batter.
  5. Cook until bubbles appear, flip and cook until golden on both sides.
  6. Serve warm with berries or honey.
Breakfast

Avocado Toast

Crunchy toast topped with avocado and eggs.

10 min Easy 2 servings
View full recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of bread
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Toast the bread until crisp.
  2. Mash the avocado with lemon juice, salt and pepper.
  3. Heat olive oil in a non-stick pan and cook the eggs to your preference.
  4. Spread the avocado on the toast.
  5. Add an egg to each slice and serve immediately.

Fresh Garden Salad

Fresh vegetables tossed in a bright lemon dressing.

15 min Easy 2 servings
View full recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cucumber
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 carrot
  • 4 lettuce leaves
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Wash and dry all the vegetables.
  2. Slice the cucumber and tomatoes; grate the carrot.
  3. Tear the lettuce and add it to a large bowl with the other vegetables.
  4. Whisk olive oil, lemon juice, salt and pepper together.
  5. Pour over the salad, toss gently and serve.

Vegetable Fried Rice

A quick lunch using cooked rice and colorful vegetables.

25 min Easy 2 servings
View full recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked and cooled rice
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 1/2 bell pepper, diced
  • 1/2 cup green peas
  • 1/2 onion, chopped
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil

Instructions

  1. Prepare the vegetables and use previously cooked, properly chilled rice.
  2. Heat the oil in a large pan over medium-high heat.
  3. Cook the onion and carrot for 3 minutes, then add the pepper and peas.
  4. Add the rice and stir-fry until thoroughly heated.
  5. Stir in the soy sauce and serve hot.

Creamy Mushroom Pasta

Comforting pasta coated in a silky mushroom sauce.

25 min Medium 2 servings
View full recipe

Ingredients

  • 200 g pasta
  • 150 g mushrooms, sliced
  • 100 ml cooking cream
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • Grated cheese, optional

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta in salted water until al dente; reserve a little pasta water.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan and sauté mushrooms until golden.
  3. Add garlic and cook for 30 seconds, then stir in the cream.
  4. Season the sauce with salt and pepper.
  5. Add the drained pasta and a splash of pasta water; toss until coated.
  6. Serve warm with grated cheese if you like.

Baked Salmon

Tender salmon with fresh lemon and herbs.

30 min Medium 2 servings
View full recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 salmon fillets (about 150 g each)
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1 tsp dried herbs
  • Salt and pepper
  • Vegetables to serve

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C and line a baking dish.
  2. Place the fillets in the dish and drizzle with olive oil and lemon juice.
  3. Sprinkle with garlic, herbs, salt and pepper.
  4. Bake for approximately 12–15 minutes, depending on thickness, until cooked through.
  5. Serve with vegetables and lemon wedges.

Homemade Cookies

Golden cookies with soft centers and chocolate chips.

30 min Easy 12 cookies
View full recipe

Ingredients

  • 150 g all-purpose flour
  • 100 g softened butter
  • 80 g sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 80 g chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C and line a baking sheet.
  2. Cream the butter and sugar, then beat in the egg.
  3. Stir in the flour and baking soda until just combined.
  4. Fold in the chocolate chips and spoon 12 portions onto the sheet.
  5. Bake for 10–12 minutes until the edges are golden.
  6. Let cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes before moving.

Creamy yogurt layered with berries and crunchy granola.

10 min Easy 2 servings
View full recipe

Ingredients

  • 200 g Greek yogurt
  • 100 g fresh berries
  • 60 g granola
  • 1 tsp honey, optional

Instructions

Pasta Love: Little Tips, Big Flavor

Our featured pasta deserves a little extra attention. Try these three chef's tips to bring more flavor and a smoother texture to your next dinner.

Dinner · 25 minutes

Creamy Mushroom Pasta

The full recipe is in the Dinner section above. Here are three extra cooking tips to make your pasta taste even better.

Chef's tips

01
Brown the mushrooms

Cook them in a hot pan without crowding them so they develop a rich, savory flavor.

02
Save pasta water

Add a little starchy cooking water when mixing the pasta and sauce for a smoother finish.

03
Finish on low heat

Once you add the cream, keep the heat gentle so the sauce stays silky.

Serving idea: Add fresh parsley, black pepper and a little grated cheese just before serving.
