Breakfast

Fluffy Pancakes

Soft golden pancakes for a cozy morning.

20 min Easy 2 servings
View full recipe

Ingredients

  • 125 g all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 180 ml milk
  • 1 tbsp melted butter, plus extra for the pan
  • Berries or honey to serve

Instructions

  1. Combine flour, sugar and baking powder in a bowl.
  2. Whisk the egg, milk and melted butter in another bowl.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and mix gently.
  4. Heat a lightly buttered pan over medium heat and add small portions of batter.
  5. Cook until bubbles appear, flip and cook until golden on both sides.
  6. Serve warm with berries or honey.

Avocado Toast

Crunchy toast topped with avocado and eggs.

10 min Easy 2 servings
View full recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of bread
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Toast the bread until crisp.
  2. Mash the avocado with lemon juice, salt and pepper.
  3. Heat olive oil in a non-stick pan and cook the eggs to your preference.
  4. Spread the avocado on the toast.
  5. Add an egg to each slice and serve immediately.

Lagman

A quick lunch using cooked rice and colorful vegetables.

45 min Easy 2 servings
View full recipe

Ingredients

  • 250 g noodles
  • 200 g beef
  • 1 carrot
  • 1/2 bell pepper
  • 1/2 onion, chopped
  • 1 tomato
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • Salt, pepper, and spices

Instructions

  1. Boil the noodles in salted water until tender, then drain and rinse.
  2. Heat the vegetable oil in a large pan over high heat and brown the meat..
  3. Add the onion and carrot, cook for 3–4 minutes, then add the bell pepper and garlic.
  4. Stir in the tomato paste, tomatoes, and spices, and simmer on medium heat for about 10 minutes.
  5. Place the noodles on a plate, top with the meat and vegetable sauce, and serve hot.
Breakfast

Baursak

Soft, golden pieces of fried dough, often served with tea.

2 hr Medium 8 servings
View full recipe

Ingredients

  • 500 g all-purpose flour
  • 250 ml warm milk
  • 7 g dry yeast
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • Vegetable oil for deep-frying

Instructions

  1. Mix the warm milk, yeast and sugar. Leave for 10 minutes until foamy.
  2. Add the flour, salt and 2 tablespoons of oil. Knead into a soft dough.
  3. Cover and leave in a warm place for 60–90 minutes until the dough rises.
  4. Roll the dough to about 1 cm thick and cut into small squares or diamonds.
  5. Let the pieces rest for 10 minutes while heating frying oil to about 175°C.
  6. Fry in batches until golden on both sides. Drain on paper towels and serve warm.
Breakfast

Shelpek

Thin fried flatbreads served warm with tea or honey.

45 min Easy 7 flatbreads
View full recipe

Ingredients

  • 400 g all-purpose flour
  • 200 ml warm water or milk
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil for the dough
  • Vegetable oil for frying
  • Honey or butter to serve (optional)

Instructions

  1. Combine the flour and salt in a bowl.
  2. Add the warm water or milk and a tablespoon of oil. Knead until smooth.
  3. Cover the dough and let it rest for 20 minutes.
  4. Divide it into seven pieces and roll each piece into a thin circle.
  5. Heat a little oil in a frying pan and cook each flatbread on both sides until lightly golden.
  6. Drain briefly and serve warm.

Beshbarmak

Tender meat over wide homemade noodles with onion broth.

3 hr 30 min Medium 6 servings
View full recipe

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg beef or lamb on the bone
  • 400 g all-purpose flour
  • 1 egg
  • 150 ml water for the dough
  • 3 onions
  • 3 litres water for the broth
  • Salt and black pepper

Instructions

  1. Put the meat in a large pot of cold water and bring to a boil. Skim the foam.
  2. Add one onion and salt, then simmer for 2–3 hours until the meat is tender. Keep the broth.
  3. Knead the flour, egg, 150 ml water and a little salt into a firm dough. Rest for 30 minutes.
  4. Roll the dough thinly and cut it into large squares or wide strips.
  5. Slice the remaining onions and soften them in a little hot broth with black pepper.
  6. Boil the dough pieces in the meat broth until cooked.
  7. Arrange the noodles on a platter, add sliced meat and onions, and serve with broth on the side.
Dinner

Kuyrdak

Pan-fried lamb, liver, potatoes and onions.

1 hr Medium 4 servings
View full recipe

Ingredients

  • 400 g lamb, diced
  • 200 g lamb or beef liver, diced
  • 3 medium potatoes
  • 2 onions
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil or rendered fat
  • 100 ml water or broth
  • Salt and black pepper

Instructions

  1. Cut the lamb, liver and potatoes into bite-sized pieces; slice the onions.
  2. Heat the oil or fat in a large pan and brown the lamb.
  3. Add the onions and cook until they begin to soften.
  4. Add the potatoes and broth. Cover and simmer until the potatoes and lamb are nearly tender.
  5. Add the liver near the end and cook it thoroughly, stirring gently.
  6. Season with salt and pepper. Serve hot.
