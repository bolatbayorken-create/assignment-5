1. Revive Wilted Herbs

Soak limp cilantro, parsley, or lettuce in a bowl of ice water with a splash of lemon juice for 15-30 minutes to make them crisp again.

2. Fix Over-Salted Soup

Drop a peeled raw potato or a small amount of rice into the pot. It will absorb excess salt while simmering. Remove before serving.

3. Ripen Avocados Fast

Place an unripe avocado in a brown paper bag with a banana or an apple. Natural ethylene gas released by the fruit accelerates ripening.

4. Prevent Onion Tears

Chill the onion in the refrigerator for 30 minutes before cutting. Lower temperatures slow down the release of the enzyme that causes crying.

5. Test Egg Freshness

Submerge eggs in a bowl of cold water. If they sink flat to the bottom, they are very fresh. If they stand up or float, they are old.

6. Soften Hard Brown Sugar

Place hardened brown sugar in a microwave-safe bowl with a damp paper towel and microwave in 20-second bursts until soft.
